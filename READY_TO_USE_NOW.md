## 🔗 HOW TO ADD PEOPLE TO YOUR SQUAD (YOUR QUESTION!)

### **NEW FEATURE: Invite Links!** ✨

1. **Create your squad** (Dashboard → Create Squad)

2. **Open your squad** (Click the squad card from dashboard)

3. **Click the "Invite Link" button** (🔗 icon in the hero section)

4. **Copy the link:**
   ```
   Example: http://localhost:3000/join/abc123-def456-ghi789
   ```

5. **Share it anywhere:**
   - WhatsApp
   - Discord  
   - Telegram
   - Email
   - SMS
   - Social Media

6. **Your friends click the link:**
   - They see squad name, description, members
   - Click "Join Squad" button
   - ✅ Done! They're in your squad!

### **For Mobile Users:**

Share this format:
```
http://YOUR_IP:3000/join/squad-id
Example: http://192.168.1.5:3000/join/abc123...
```

**How to find YOUR_IP:** Run `ipconfig` on your computer
